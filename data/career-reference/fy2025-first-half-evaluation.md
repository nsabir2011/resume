### MBO drafting lessons

- Use evidence completed inside the exact evaluation period.
- Define goals whose measurement is controlled by the employee; avoid relying on informal CSAT unless a collection method exists.
- Track delivery dates, estimation deviation, KPT completion, and stakeholder approval as work happens.
- Distinguish a draft or explanation from a completed reusable knowledge asset.
- Record project ideas formally in Jira or another accepted system within the period.

### Contribution drafting lessons

- Avoid claiming the same action in both competency and contribution categories unless it creates a clearly separate organizational outcome.
- Prefer direct evidence for recruitment and business creation over adjacent or potential future value.
- Describe the organizational mechanism and result: who adopted the work, what changed, and how the company benefited.
