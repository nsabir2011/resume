# Accepted Portfolio Baseline

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

**Accepted:** 2026-08-12

**Source mockup:** [`profile-led/index.html`](profile-led/index.html)

The `profile-led` concept is the visual and information-architecture baseline for the actual portfolio website. It replaces the earlier Web CV, project-story, and visual-direction experiments as the primary reference.

## Why this direction was selected

- It presents career experience clearly without looking like a conventional CV.
- The introduction is restrained and identifies the professional role without a large marketing headline.
- LinkedIn and GitHub sit directly beneath the portrait area.
- The About section is concise and capability-focused rather than promotional.
- Experience is easy to scan through structured employer entries.
- Projects describe what Nazmus contributed without disclosing project intricacies.
- Skills are grouped into meaningful technical categories.
- Publications, mentoring, education, and certification remain visible without competing with experience and projects.

## Implementation guardrails

- Keep the small `AI/ML Engineer · Technical Lead` role label.
- Avoid oversized slogans, sales copy, freelancing language, and customer-acquisition calls to action.
- Prefer neutral, evidence-based phrasing and minimize first-person copy.
- Keep project descriptions at overview level: purpose or category, personal contribution, period, and relevant technologies.
- Do not expose client names, internal architecture, unpublished patent details, or confidential implementation methods.
- Use anonymized public project labels from `data/career-reference/sources-and-publication.md`.
- Keep experience, projects, skills, public work, mentoring, credentials, and contact as the main content flow.
- Only add testimonials when genuine quotes and attribution are supplied and approved.
