# Portfolio Homepage Design Directions

These are local static drafts for choosing a visual direction. They are not yet publication-ready; confidential project details and metrics must be reviewed before deployment.

## Accepted baseline

The **Profile-Led Portfolio** in [`profile-led/index.html`](profile-led/index.html) was accepted on 2026-08-12 as the baseline for the actual website.

It combines a restrained professional introduction, portrait with LinkedIn and GitHub, concise About section, structured experience, overview-only projects, categorized skills, public work, mentoring, and credentials. The implementation decisions and remaining inputs are recorded in [`BASELINE.md`](BASELINE.md).

The four directions below are retained as earlier visual experiments rather than implementation candidates.

## 1. Precision Dark

- **Mood:** Technical, confident, modern, high-contrast
- **Visual language:** Near-black surfaces, cyan signal color, monospace labels, crisp grid
- **Content emphasis:** Engineering outcomes, project constraints, measurable impact
- **Best fit:** Presenting Nazmus primarily as a senior AI engineer and technical lead
- **Tradeoff:** More overtly "tech portfolio" and less personal/editorial

## 2. Editorial Light

- **Mood:** Thoughtful, mature, research-oriented
- **Visual language:** Warm paper background, serif display typography, black/red editorial accents
- **Content emphasis:** Ideas, writing, project narratives, technical judgment
- **Best fit:** Balancing engineering depth with research, authorship, and mentoring
- **Tradeoff:** Metrics feel less dominant than in Precision Dark

## 3. Blueprint Tech

- **Mood:** Systems-minded, inventive, architectural
- **Visual language:** Blueprint grid, electric blue, orange callouts, diagram-like project organization
- **Content emphasis:** Pipelines, constraints, ownership, progression from research to deployment
- **Best fit:** Showing complex end-to-end engineering and system design
- **Tradeoff:** Strong visual personality may compete with longer written content

## 4. Warm Minimal

- **Mood:** Calm, approachable, premium, human
- **Visual language:** Spacious cream canvas, deep green, restrained typography, soft shapes
- **Content emphasis:** Selected work, leadership, mentoring, and clear storytelling
- **Best fit:** A broadly appealing professional portfolio for technical and non-technical visitors
- **Tradeoff:** Less visibly experimental than Blueprint Tech or Precision Dark

## Shared homepage content

All four directions use the same core story:

- AI/ML engineer and technical lead at Chowa Giken since October 2022
- Work spanning computer vision, generative AI, multimodal RAG, full-stack delivery, and cloud deployment
- Selected anonymized case studies: MRAG, inference optimization, explainable forecasting, and generative floorplans
- Public Chowa Giken articles and demo pages
- Lecturer-equivalent university mentoring
- Email and LinkedIn contact routes

## Recommended decision criteria

Choose based on the audience you most want to impress:

- **Engineering leaders:** Precision Dark or Blueprint Tech
- **Research and innovation teams:** Editorial Light or Blueprint Tech
- **Mixed recruiters, clients, and executives:** Warm Minimal
- **Personal preference and memorability:** Select the direction that feels most like you, then refine its copy and project depth
