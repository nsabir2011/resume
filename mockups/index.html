Computer vision · Optimization

3.2× faster inference, without retraining

A high-resolution 360° image pipeline split every sample into four parts because the complete image could not fit in 16 GB of GPU memory.

Challenge
Reduce processing time while preserving the existing model and its outputs.
My role
Analyzed and optimized inference execution and data movement between pipeline stages.
Outcome
Runtime fell from about 42 seconds to 12–13 seconds per sample with unchanged outputs.

Generative vision · Research to delivery

Generating floorplans under hard model constraints

Commercial-use and model-origin requirements ruled out many newer systems, while the available Stable Diffusion 1.5 model and limited client data made direct generation unreliable.

Approach
Split generation into color segments, walls, inpainting, and image-processing stages; later evaluate a viable newer model.
My role
Owned color-segment generation, contributed annotation, built the REST API and deployment, then led the newer-model feasibility study.
Outcome
Delivered the constrained pipeline and demonstrated that a newer 4B model could generate the final floorplan directly.
